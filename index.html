Mayari’s Reminder

Pick a Moon

If this little collection ever finds you on a difficult day, I hope it reminds you that somewhere in the middle of ordinary life, someone took the time to make something gentle for you.

Not because you had to earn it. Not because you needed to give anything back. Not because I expected you to have the perfect response to it.

I just liked the thought of leaving a lil warmth somewhere you could return to whenever you needed it.

So whenever you tap one of these moons, I hope you find at least one thing that makes the day feel a lil lighter. Maybe a reminder you needed. Maybe something that makes you laugh. Maybe just a small moment where you feel a lil less alone with whatever you’re carrying.

That’s all I wanted this to be, woman. Something kind, made with care, that you can keep coming back to whenever you need it.
